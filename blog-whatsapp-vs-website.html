WhatsApp Business vs a Real Website: Why You Need Both

By Jonabby & Co. Enterprises · September 26, 2026

"But I already have WhatsApp Business — why do I need a website too?" We hear this a lot, and it's a fair question. The honest answer is that WhatsApp and a website do two completely different jobs. Neither one replaces the other.

WhatsApp is where relationships happen

WhatsApp is personal, fast, and familiar — which is exactly why Nigerians love it. It's perfect for answering questions, negotiating, sending payment details, and closing a sale one-on-one. There's no better tool for that final, human step of a purchase.

But WhatsApp can't be found — a website can

Nobody discovers your business by randomly guessing your WhatsApp number. They find you through Google search, a shared link, or word of mouth — and then they check you out online before they message. If there's nothing to check, many will simply move on to a competitor who does show up.

A website pre-sells, so your DMs get warmer leads

Without a website, every single WhatsApp message you get starts from zero: "What do you sell? How much? Where are you based?" With a website, customers arrive already knowing your prices, your products, and why you're worth buying from — so the conversation moves straight to "I want to order," not "tell me about your business."

A website works while you're asleep, on transport, or fully booked

You can only reply to WhatsApp when you're free. Your website never sleeps. It keeps presenting your business, answering questions, and collecting interested customers 24 hours a day — so you never lose a sale just because you didn't see a message in time.

The winning combination

The businesses that grow fastest in Nigeria right now use both together: a website that builds trust and answers questions upfront, with a prominent WhatsApp button so the customer can jump straight into a real conversation the moment they're ready to buy. That's exactly how every site we build is designed to work.
