Every December, Lagos transforms. Diaspora Nigerians fly home in their thousands, owambe season goes into overdrive, weddings and parties book out venues months in advance, and everyone from caterers to fashion designers to event planners gets flooded with more customers than the rest of the year combined.

"The businesses that show up online before the season starts are the ones who capture it."

Why "Detty December" changes how customers search

Returning diaspora and out-of-town visitors don't have an existing network of vendors they trust — they Google, they check Instagram, they ask in WhatsApp groups, and they book whoever looks most professional and easiest to reach. A business with no website is invisible to that entire wave of first-time customers, no matter how good the product is.

Why now is the exact window to act

A website built in the last week of November barely has time to get noticed before the season is already in full swing. That's exactly why entries for this contest close October 30 — early enough that the winner's site can be planned, built, and already showing up on Google well before the December rush starts, instead of racing to catch up once it's underway.
